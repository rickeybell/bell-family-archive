William “Dickey” Bell — First Grade Report Card, Erwin School, 1962–1963

Circa June 1963
Erwin Elementary School, Lancaster County, SC
Dickey B

First-grade report card for William “Dickey” Bell from Erwin School, Lancaster City Schools, Lancaster, South Carolina, for the 1962–1963 school year. His teacher was Nancy L. Graham, and the principal was Mrs. T. K. Cunningham. At the end of the year, Dickey was assigned to second grade. Dickey earned A grades in Arithmetic, Reading, Writing, Science, and Health, with a final citizenship grade of B. The report records 180 days present, no absences, and no tardies for the school year. His habits and attitudes were marked satisfactory in nearly every category, including completing his work, following directions, respecting others, working independently, and caring for books and property. The one recurring area marked “Needs Improvement” was controlling talking and whispering. The report card contains several parent signatures of James S. Bell, providing an additional family record connected with Dickey's elementary-school years.

Farm League Tigers Defeat Dickey Bell’s Yankees, 18–12

June 20, 1963
Dickey B

Newspaper article reporting a Farm League baseball game at Hunter Field in which the Tigers defeated the Yankees, 18–12. The Tigers, managed by Don Scott and Dick Richards, earned their second consecutive victory after losing their first eleven games. Joe Simpson was the winning pitcher, while Sam Thomas, Randy Watts, and Gary Powell led the Tigers at the plate. Ray Bowers and Larry Blackwell were the leading Yankee hitters. The Yankees were coached by Bob Ackerman. Following the loss, the coaches and team were invited to Dickey Bell’s home, where they were treated to hot dogs to “soothe aching souls and wounded pride.”

Farm League Yankees Close Out Season — Dickey Bell Hosts Team Party

Circa July 1963
Dickey B

Newspaper article describing the final weeks of the Farm League Yankees' baseball season. The Yankees defeated the Red Sox 2–1 on Tuesday and defeated the Tigers twice, 4–0 on Wednesday and 6–2 on Thursday. On Wednesday night, Dickey Bell hosted a hot dog party at his home for the Yankees. Following Thursday's game, the team celebrated Ronnie Rogers' surprise birthday with Pepsi, cake, and ice cream. The article also records several team outings organized by Coach Bob Ackerman. On June 26, he took the players to Charlotte, North Carolina, to watch live championship wrestling at WBTV. The Yankees then planned a trip to Atlanta on July 6 and 7 to see the Atlanta Braves play.

William “Dickey” Bell — Third Grade Report Card, Erwin School, 1964–1965

Circa June 1965
Erwin Elementary School, Lancaster County, SC
Dickey B

Third-grade report card for William “Dickey” Bell from Erwin School, Lancaster City Schools, Lancaster, South Carolina, for the 1964–1965 school year. His teacher was Gladys R. Sims. At the completion of the school year, Dickey was assigned to fourth grade. Dickey had an exceptionally strong academic year, earning A's throughout the year in Arithmetic, Reading, Language, Writing, Spelling, Social Studies, Science, and Health. His year-end marks in these academic subjects were all A's. Citizenship was also consistently graded A. The report records 180 days present with no absences, giving Dickey perfect attendance for the school year. His habits and attitudes were marked satisfactory throughout, including courtesy, attentiveness, working independently, respecting authority, completing assignments, and working well with others. The report card contains repeated parent signatures of James S. Bell, including a more formal signature on the final report.

William “Dickey” Bell — Fourth Grade Report Card, Erwin School, 1965–1966

Circa June 1966
Erwin Elementary School, Lancaster County, SC
Dickey B

Fourth-grade report card for William “Dickey” Bell from Erwin School, Lancaster City Schools, Lancaster, South Carolina, for the 1965–1966 school year. His teacher was Julia R. Beckham. At the end of the school year, Dickey was promoted to fifth grade. Dickey continued to perform strongly academically, receiving predominantly A and B grades in Arithmetic, Reading, Language, Writing, Spelling, Social Studies/Geography, Science, and Health. His attendance was excellent, with the report showing 180 days present for the school year. The habits-and-attitudes section contains the handwritten teacher comment “Talks too much”. Early reporting periods also show areas needing improvement in interrupting, neatness, and attentiveness, with improvement evident later in the year. The report card contains several signatures of his father, James Stewart Bell Jr. The final report includes the humorous handwritten parental comment “That’s a laugh!” followed by his BROTHER's full signature.

Dickey Bell Receives Statewide Honorable Mention in Conservation Essay Contest — May 27, 1968

May 27, 1968
Lancaster County, SC
Dickey B

Award check issued to Dickey Bell on May 27, 1968, recognizing his achievement in a statewide Soil and Water Conservation essay contest. Dickey was named one of fifteen Honorable Mention winners statewide and received a $5.00 award. The check was issued through The Bank of Lancaster by the Lancaster Soil & Water Conservation District and signed by James M. Kirk, Treasurer. This award followed Dickey's earlier second-place local essay contest award on March 21, 1968, for which he received $10. His essay therefore advanced beyond the Lancaster competition and earned recognition at the statewide level.
