Family memory — The Buck Stove, early 1980s:
Dad was in his wild-child mode in the early 1980s. One night he didn't come home, which wasn't particularly unusual at the time. Granddad Buster was mad because Dad was supposed to help cut wood for the Buck stove in the living room. Buster called Dad's friend Donald Barrett looking for him. Donald said Dad was there asleep and would not get up. Buster told Donald, in not-polite terms, to throw water on his A** and wake him up because he had stuff to do.
Family recollection — Rickey Bell.
