1940

Alma Tharp Receives Private Pilot License

Circa 1940
Charleston, SC
Alma B

Newspaper clipping reporting that Alma Tharp of Folly Beach, South Carolina, qualified for her private pilot license at Carolina Skyways Airport. William S. Scott was president of Carolina Skyways. Alma subsequently became a member of the Ninety-Nines; an October 1946 Ninety-Nines publication identifies her as a new member and reports a meeting held at her Folly Beach home.

1948

Alma Tharp Flies to Carolina Ninety-Nines Annual Convention

Circa 1948
Charleston, SC
Alma B

Alma Tharp flies to Carolinas Ninety-Nines convention, Spartanburg, S.C., circa 1948. Newspaper report on the two-day annual convention of the Carolinas Chapter of the Ninety-Nines. Alma Tharp flew from Charleston to Spartanburg in her nearly new Ercoupe. Other attendees included Jewell Bailey, Lucille Greenwood, Louise Smith, Bird Eaton, Emalie Stone and Edna Hartness. Exact publication date remains under investigation.

Carolina Ninety-Nines Annual Meeting in Spartanburg

Circa 1948
Charleston, SC
Alma B

Circa 1948 — Spartanburg, South Carolina. Newspaper announcement of the annual meeting of the Carolinas Chapter of the Ninety-Nines, the international organization of women pilots. Members were scheduled to gather in Spartanburg for a weekend meeting, including a buffet supper and the chapter's annual business meeting at the Cleveland Hotel. The article lists “Alma Thorpe” of Charleston, apparently a newspaper misspelling of Alma Tharp, along with fellow Charleston pilot Jewell Bailey. Other expected attendees included Emalie Stone, Edna Hartness, Louise Smith, Bird Eaton, Lucille Greene, Anne Fishburne and Vera Self.

Alma Tharp with Her Ercoupe

Circa 1948
Alma B

Circa 1948. Alma Tharp, at left, with another woman beside an Ercoupe aircraft. Alma was an active member of the Carolinas Chapter of the Ninety-Nines and a licensed private pilot. A contemporary newspaper account records Alma flying her nearly new Ercoupe from Charleston to Spartanburg for a Carolinas Ninety-Nines convention. Exact date and location of this photograph have not yet been confirmed.

Alma Tharp Attends Ninety-Nines Meeting for 1950 Women’s Transcontinental Air Race

Circa May 1950
Charleston, SC
Alma B

1950 — Charleston, South Carolina. Alma Tharp of Folly Beach attended a quarterly meeting of the Carolinas Chapter of the Ninety-Nines at Charleston Municipal Airport. Members discussed plans for the annual All-Woman Transcontinental Air Race, scheduled to begin in San Diego on June 11 and finish in Greenville, South Carolina, with Charleston serving as the eastern continental marker. The article lists Alma among the Carolinas Ninety-Nines members attending the meeting.
