Technical Solutions – Bell Family Business – 1998

July 26, 1998
Dickey B, Rickey B, Stephanie B

July 26, 1998 Lancaster News feature on Technical Solutions, a Lancaster business equipment and computer company founded October 14, 1996, by Ricky Bell, Stephanie Bell, and Dickey Bell. The company sold and serviced Sharp copiers and fax machines and provided custom-built computers, networking, upgrades, and computer repair. The article also lists Ricky Bell as a service technician and Debbie Phillips as a member of the sales staff.
