KSSI – Fly-In BBQ

May 3, 2024
Mike Morgan, Sabrina Odem

KSSI – Fly-In BBQ Sometimes the best flying destinations are the simple ones: an airplane, a runway, and good BBQ. A flight into KSSI – St. Simons Island Airport combines the fun of flying with one of aviation’s favorite traditions—going somewhere just because there’s something good to eat when you get there. Land the airplane, tie it down, grab some BBQ, enjoy St. Simons Island for a while, and then climb back in for the flight home. Because pilots will fly a long way for good BBQ.
